NDA 219083/S-001 SUPPLEMENT APPROVAL Cytokinetics, Incorporated Attention: Tiffany Wan, MBS Senior Director, Regulatory Affairs 350 Oyster Point Boulevard South San Fransico, CA 94080 Dear Tiffany Wan: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received January 7, 2026, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Myqorzo (aficamten) tablets. This Changes Being Effected supplemental new drug application provides for proposed modifications to the approved Myqorzo risk evaluation and mitigation strategy (REMS). RISK EVALUATION AND MITIGATION STRATEGY (REMS) REQUIREMENTS The REMS for Myqorzo was originally approved on December 19, 2025. The REMS consists of elements to assure safe use, an implementation system, and a timetable for submission of assessments of the REMS. Your proposed modification to the REMS consists of: • Modifying the language in REMS materials regarding treatment interruptions for left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) 40% to <50% to align with the approved label. • Modifying the indication statement in REMS materials to align with the indication in the approved label. • Editorial revisions to the REMS Document and other REMS materials. Your proposed modified REMS, submitted on January 7, 2026, amended and appended to this letter, is approved. The timetable for submission of assessments of the REMS remains the same as that approved on December 19, 2025. There are no changes to the REMS assessment plan described in our December 19, 2025 letter.
