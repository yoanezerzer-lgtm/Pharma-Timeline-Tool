Attention: Michelle Sanders, MS Director, Global Regulatory Affairs 2440 Research Boulevard Rockville, MD 20850 Dear Michelle Sanders: Please refer to your new drug application (NDA) received November 24, 2025, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Simtriyo (centanafadine) extended-release capsules. This NDA provides for the use of Simtriyo (centanafadine) extended-release capsules for the treatment of attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) in adults and pediatric patients 6 years of age and older weighing at least 20 kg. APPROVAL & LABELING We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling. CONTROLLED SUBSTANCE SCHEDULING You were previously informed that FDA intends to recommend scheduling of centanafadine under the Controlled Substances Act (CSA). The scheduling of this product in accordance with the CSA (21 U.S.C. 811) is not yet complete as of the date of this letter. Therefore, in accordance with the FDCA (21 U.S.C. 355(x)), the date of approval for Simtriyo shall be the date on which the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) publishes a notice in the Federal Register announcing the interim final scheduling of centanafadine. We note that, when the drug is scheduled by the DEA, you will need to make appropriate revisions to the Prescribing Information, Medication Guide, and carton and container labeling by submitting a supplement to your NDA. This would include the statements in the labeling detailing the scheduling of centanafadine, as the scheduled substance in Simtriyo, as required under 21 CFR 201.57(a)(2) and (c)(10)(i). DATING PERIOD Based on the stability data submitted to date, the expiry dating period for Simtriyo (centanafadine) extended-release capsule shall be 24 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature). Results of ongoing stability studies should be submitted throughout the dating period in your annual report , as they become available, including the results of stability studies from the first three production lots. ADVISORY COMMITTEE Your application for Simtriyo was not referred to an FDA advisory committee because the clinical trial design is acceptable and the application did not raise significant safety or efficacy issues in the intended population. REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA; 21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable. We are waiving the pediatric studies requirement for ages 0 to 3 years because necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable. This is because symptoms of ADHD are considered difficult to distinguish from highly variable normative behaviors before the age of 4 years. We are waiving the pediatric studies requirement for ages 4 to 5 years because there is evidence strongly suggesting that the drug product would be unsafe in this pediatric group. This pediatric subpopulation had a higher incidence of weight loss than pediatric patients 6 years of age and older. We note that you have fulfilled the pediatric study requirement for ages 6 to 17 years for this application. NDA 218145 Page 4 POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENTS UNDER 505(o) Section 505(o)(3) of the FDCA authorizes FDA to require holders of approved drug and biological product applications to conduct postmarketing studies and clinical trials for certain purposes, if FDA makes certain findings required by the statute. We have determined that an analysis of spontaneous postmarketing adverse events reported under subsection 505(k)(1) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess a signal of a serious risk of altered safety and pharmacokinetics of centanafadine in patients with severe hepatic impairment, or to identify unexpected serious risks associated with exposure to centanafadine during lactation and pregnancy. Furthermore, the active postmarket risk identification and analysis system as available under section 505(k)(3) of the FDCA will not be sufficient to assess these serious risks. Therefore, based on appropriate scientific data, FDA has determined that you are required to conduct the following studies: 5035-1 Evaluate the effect of severe hepatic impairment on safety and pharmacokinetics of centanafadine. The timetable you submitted on July 9, 2026, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule: Draft Protocol Submission: 12/2026 Final Protocol Submission: 06/2027 Study Completion: 10/2028 Final Report Submission: 04/2029 5035-2 Perform a lactation study in lactating women who have received therapeutic doses of centanafadine to evaluate the pharmacokinetics of centanafadine and its major metabolites in breast milk, using a validated assay. Evaluate the effects of centanafadine on the breastfed infant, if available, based on study population. The timetable you submitted on July 8, 2026, states that you will conduct this study according to the following schedule: Draft Protocol Submission: 02/2027 Final Protocol Submission: 08/2027 Study Completion: 08/2029 Final Report Submission: 02/2030 U.S. Food and Drug Administration Silver Spring, MD 20993 www.fda.gov Reference ID: 5841185
