This Prior Approval supplemental biologics license application provides for a new indication for Keytruda, in combination with enfortumab vedotin, as neoadjuvant treatment, and then continued after cystectomy as adjuvant treatment for the treatment of adult patients with muscle invasive bladder cancer who are ineligible for cisplatin - containing chemotherapy. REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable. We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application because studies would be impossible or highly impracticable.
