Attention: Lynn May Brown, PhD Director, Global Regulatory Affairs 1 Merck Dr., PO Box 100 Whitehouse Station, NJ 08889 Dear Dr. Brown: Please refer to your supplemental biologics license applications (sBLA), dated August 20, 2021, and your amendments, submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for Keytruda (pembrolizumab) Injection. We also refer to your supplemental biologics license application 034 (S-034), approved on June 12, 2018, under the regulations at 21 CFR 601 Subpart E for Accelerated Approval of Biological Products for Serious or Life-Threatening Illnesses. Prior Approval supplemental biologics application 121 (S-121) provides for the use of Keytruda, in combination with chemotherapy, with or without bevacizumab, for the treatment of patients with persistent, recurrent, or metastatic cervical cancer whose tumors express PD-L1 (CPS ≥1) as determined by an FDA -approved test. Prior Approval supplemental biologics application 122 (S-122) provides for regular approval of Keytruda, as a single agent, for the treatment of patients with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer with disease progression on or after chemotherapy whose tumors express PD-L1 (CPS ≥1) as determined by an FDA -approved test. They are approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed- upon labeling.
