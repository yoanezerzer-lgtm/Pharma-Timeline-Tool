This Prior Approval supplemental biologics license application updates the Prescribing Information regarding the selection of patients with • Esophageal or gastroesophageal junction cancer based on PD-L1 (CPS ≥1) expression as determined by an FDA-authorized test; and • Inclusion of specific companion diagnostic assays used in clinical trials for gastric cancer (in KEYNOTE-859 and KEYNOTE-811) and cervical cancer (in KEYNOTE-826) in Sections 14.10 and 14.12, respectively.
