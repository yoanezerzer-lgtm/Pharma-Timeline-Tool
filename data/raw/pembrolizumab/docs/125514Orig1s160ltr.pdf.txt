BLA 125514/S-160 SUPPLEMENT APPROVAL Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC Attention: Corina Nolasco, MSc, MBA Director, Global Regulatory Affairs 126 East Lincoln Ave Rahway, NJ 07065 Dear Corina Nolasco: Please refer to your supplemental biologics license application (sBLA), dated March 1, 2024, received March 1, 2024, submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for Keytruda (pembrolizumab) injection. We also refer to our letter dated February 1, 2024, notifying you, under Section 505(o)(4) of the FDCA, of new safety information that pertains to the risk of transplant (including corneal graft) rejection, that we have determined should be included in the labeling for the class of immune checkpoint inhibitors. This supplemental biologics license application provides for revisions to the labeling for Keytruda (pembrolizumab), consistent with our February 1, 2024, Safety Labeling Change Notification Letter. Corresponding revisions were made to Section 17 Patient Counseling Information and the Medication Guide was revised to reflect the changes in the Full Prescribing Information. APPROVAL & LABELING We have completed our review of this application. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling. REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable.
