REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable. We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application because the necessary studies are impossible or highly impracticable as metastatic cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma does not occur in children. POSTMARKETING COMMITMENTS SUBJECT TO REPORTING REQUIREMENTS UNDER SECTION 506B We remind you of your postmarketing commitments: 3876-1 Submit the final report and datasets from Keynote-629 evaluating overall response rate and duration of response in patients with locally advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma and those with recurrent or metastatic cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma, that may inform product labeling.
