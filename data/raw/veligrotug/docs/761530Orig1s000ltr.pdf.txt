LICENSING We have approved your BLA for Lumvoa (veligrotug-vvze) effective this date. You are hereby authorized to introduce or deliver for introduction into interstate commerce, Lumvoa under your existing Department of Health and Human Services U.S. License No. 2377. Lumvoa is indicated for treatment of thyroid eye disease. MANUFACTURING LOCATIONS Under this license, you are approved to manufacture veligrotug-vvze drug substance and drug product at WuXi Biologics Co, Ltd. (FEI 3010606982) in Mashan, Jiangsu, China. Drug product will be labeled, and secondary packaged at You may label your product with the proprietary name, Lumvoa, and market it in 500mg/10mL injection. DATING PERIOD The dating period for Lumvoa shall be 24 months from the date of manufacture when stored at 2-8 °C. The date of manufacture shall be defined as the date of final sterile filtration of the formulated drug product. The dating period for your drug substance shall be months from the date of manufacture when stored at Results of ongoing stability studies should be submitted throughout the dating period, as they become available, including the results of stability studies from the Process Performance Qualification (PPQ) lots. We have approved the stability protocols in your license application for the purpose of extending the expiration dating period of your drug substance and drug product under 21 CFR 601.12. FDA LOT RELEASE You are not currently required to submit samples of future lots of Lumvoa to the Center for Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) for release by the Director, CDER, under 21 CFR 610.2.
