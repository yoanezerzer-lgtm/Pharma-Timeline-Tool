BLA 761055/S-072 SUPPLEMENT APPROVAL Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. Attention: Elisa Babilonia, PhD Executive Director, Regulatory Affairs 777 Old Saw Mill River Road Tarrytown, NY 10591 Dear Dr. Elisa Babilonia: Please refer to your supplemental biologics license application (sBLA) received March 24, 2025, and your amendments, submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for Dupixent (dupilumab) injection. We also refer to our letter dated January 24, 2025, notifying you, under Section 505(o)(4) of the FDCA, of new safety information that we have determined should be included in the labeling for dupilumab. This information pertains to the risk of new onset psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis, and vasculitis. Other associated changes were made to the RECENT MAJOR CHANGES section of the HIGHLIGHTS OF PRESCRIBING INFORMATION. This supplemental biologics license application provides for revisions to the labeling for dupilumab. The agreed upon changes to the language included in our January 24, 2025, letter are noted in the attached label. APPROVAL & LABELING We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling. CONTENT OF LABELING As soon as possible, but no later than 14 days from the date of this letter, submit, via the FDA automated drug registration and listing system (eLIST), the content of labeling [21 CFR 601.14(b)] in structured product labeling (SPL) format, as described at FDA.gov, 1 that is identical to the enclosed labeling (text for the Prescribing Information) and include the labeling changes proposed in any pending “Changes Being Effected” (CBE) supplements. 1 http://www.fda.gov/ForIndustry/DataStandards/StructuredProductLabeling/default.htm Reference ID: 5572933

REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable. Because none of these criteria apply to your supplement application, you are exempt from this requirement.
