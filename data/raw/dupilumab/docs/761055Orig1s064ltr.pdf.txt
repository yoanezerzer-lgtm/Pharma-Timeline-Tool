BLA 761055/S-064 SUPPLEMENT APPROVAL Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. Attention: Weronika Chappo, Pharm.D. Director, Regulatory Affairs 777 Old Saw Mill River Rd Tarrytown, NY 10591 Dear Dr. Chappo: Please refer to your supplemental biologics license application (sBLA) dated and received December 27, 2023, under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for Dupixent (dupilumab) Injection. We acknowledge receipt of your major amendment dated May 13, 2024, which extended the goal date by three months. This Prior Approval supplemental biologics license application provides for the use of Dupixent as an add-on maintenance treatment in adult patients with inadequately controlled chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and an eosinophilic phenotype. APPROVAL & LABELING We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling. WAIVER OF HIGHLIGHTS ½ PAGE LENGTH REQUIREMENT FOR HIGHLIGHTS Please note that we have previously granted a waiver of the requirements of 21 CFR 201.57(d)(8) regarding the length of Highlights of Prescribing Information. REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable. We are waiving the pediatric study requirement for this application because studies are impossible or highly impracticable as this condition rarely or never occurs in pediatrics.
