BLA 761055/S-020 SUPPLEMENT APPROVAL FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. Attention: Elisa Babilonia, PhD Associate Director, Regulatory Affairs 777 Old Saw Mill River Rd Tarrytown, NY 10579 Dear Dr. Babilonia: Please refer to your supplemental biologics license application (sBLA), dated and received November 26, 2019, and your amendments, submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for DUPIXENT (dupilumab) injection, for subcutaneous use. This Prior Approval supplemental biologics application contains the final summary report for the following postmarketing requirement listed in the March 28, 2017 BLA approval letter and provides for a new patient population: patients with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis (AD) aged ≥ 6 to < 12 years. 3183-1 Conduct a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study to investigate the efficacy and safety of dupilumab administered concomitantly with topical therapy in subjects 6 years to less than 12 years of age with severe atopic dermatitis. APPROVAL & LABELING We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling.
