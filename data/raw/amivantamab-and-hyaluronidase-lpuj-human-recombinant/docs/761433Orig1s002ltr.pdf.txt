BLA 761433/S-002 ACCELERATED APPROVAL Janssen Biotech, Inc. Attention: Jasminder Kumar, PharmD, RPh Associate Director, Global Regulatory Affairs 920 U.S. Route 202 Raritan, NJ 08869 Dear Dr. Kumar: Please refer to your supplemental biologics license application (sBLA), dated and received May 20, 2026, and your amendments, submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for Rybrevant Faspro (amivantamab and hyaluronidase-lpuj) injection. This Prior Approval sBLA provides for Rybrevant Faspro as a single agent for the treatment of adult patients with recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC) whose disease has progressed on or after platinum-based chemotherapy and an anti-PD-(L)1 antibody. Limitations of Use: Rybrevant Faspro is not indicated as treatment for patients with oropharyngeal squamous cell carcinoma (OPSCC) that is human papillomavirus (HPV)- associated (i.e., by p16 IHC).
