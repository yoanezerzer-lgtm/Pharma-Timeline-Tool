Attention: Sudhakarr Balasubramanian US Regulatory Lead 7 Giralda Farms, 2nd Floor Madison, NJ 07940 Dear Sudhakarr Balasubramanian: Please refer to your supplemental biologics license application (sBLA) received June 13, 2025, and your amendments, submitted under section 351(a) of the Public Health Service Act for Adbry (tralokinumab-ldrm) injection. This Prior Approval supplemental biologics application provides for changes to the Adverse Reactions section based on data from the clinical trial titled, “Long-term Extension Trial in Subjects with Atopic Dermatitis Who Participated in Previous Tralokinumab Trials – ECZTEND”.
