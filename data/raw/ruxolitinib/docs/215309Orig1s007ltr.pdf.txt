NDA 215309/S-007 SUPPLEMENT APPROVAL FULLFILMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT Incyte Corporation Attention: Fiona Lee Director, Global Regulatory Affairs 1801 Augustine Cut-Off Wilmington, DE 19803 Dear Fiona Lee: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated August 17, 2024 and received August 19, 2024, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Opzelura (ruxolitinib) 1.5% topical cream. This Prior Approval supplemental new drug application extends the indication for ruxolitinib 1.5% cream to pediatric patients ages 2 to less than 12 years for the topical short-term and non-continuous chronic treatment of mild to moderate atopic dermatitis in non-immunocompromised patients whose disease is not adequately controlled with topical prescription therapies or when those therapies are not advisable. APPROVAL & LABELING We have completed our review of this application. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling. This supplement provides for pediatric labeling text pursuant to the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA). This approval is in response to a PREA PMR. REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable. FULFILLMENT OF POSTMARKETING REQUIREMENT(S) We have received your submission, containing the final reports for the following PREA postmarketing requirements listed in the October 13, 2021 (corrected) approval letter. 4147-1 Conduct a randomized, double-blind, 8-week trial of ruxolitinib 1.5%, ruxolitinib 0.75%, and vehicle, followed by a 44-week long-term safety extension where vehicle subjects are randomized to either ruxolitinib 1.5% or ruxolitinib 0.75%.
