REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication(s) in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable. The pediatric study requirement for children less than 5 years of age was previously waived with the January 21, 2022, approval of BLA 761105/S-014. As discussed further below, we note that the pediatric study requirement (PMR 4206-1) for this application is fulfilled.
