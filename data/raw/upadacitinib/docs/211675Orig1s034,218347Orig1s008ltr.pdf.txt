Waukegan Road North Chicago, IL 60064 Dea Peggy Pui Ki Lo: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received April 24, 2026, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Rinvoq (upadacitinib) extended-release tablet and Rinvoq LQ (upadacitinib) oral solution. We also refer to our letter dated March 26, 2026, notifying you, under Section 505(o)(4) of the FDCA, of new safety information that we have determined should be included in the labeling for Janus kinase (JAK)/Signal Transducer and Activator of Transcription (STAT) inhibitors indicated for non-hematological conditions. This information pertains to the risk of hypoglycemia, including severe hypoglycemia, in patients with diabetes after starting JAK/STAT inhibitors. This supplemental new drug application provides for revisions to the labeling for Rinvoq (upadacitinib) extended-release tablets and Rinvoq LQ (upadacitib) oral solution consistent with our March 26, 2026, letter and subsequent discussions. APPROVAL & LABELING We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling. REQUIRED PEDIATRIC ASSESSMENTS Under the Pediatric Research Equity Act (PREA) (21 U.S.C. 355c), all applications for new active ingredients (which includes new salts and new fixed combinations), new indications, new dosage forms, new dosing regimens, or new routes of administration are required to contain an assessment of the safety and effectiveness of the product for the claimed indication in pediatric patients unless this requirement is waived, deferred, or inapplicable. Because none of these criteria apply to your supplemental application, you are exempt from this requirement.
