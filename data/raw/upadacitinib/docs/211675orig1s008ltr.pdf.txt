Waukegan Road North Chicago, IL 60064 Attention: Leilane Y Oshiro Gaglianone Senior Manager, Regulatory Affairs Dear Ms. Gaglianone: Please refer to your supplemental new drug application (sNDA) dated and received October 13, 2021, and your amendments, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) for Rinvoq (upadacitinib). We also refer to our letter dated August 23, 2021, notifying you, under Section 505(o)(4) of the FDCA, of new safety information that we believe should be included in the labeling for Janus kinase inhibitor (JAKi) products approved for inflammary conditions. This information pertains to the increased risks of major adverse cardiovascular events, malignancy, thrombosis, and mortality upon the assessment of a postmarketing safety study from another JAKi product. This supplemental new drug application provides for revisions to the labeling for Rinvoq (upadacitinib) consistent with our August 23, 2021 safety labeling change notification. APPROVAL & LABELING We have completed our review of this application, as amended. It is approved, effective on the date of this letter, for use as recommended in the enclosed agreed-upon labeling.
