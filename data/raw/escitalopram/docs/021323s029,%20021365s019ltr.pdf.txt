Attention: Maricarmen Raposo Associate, Regulatory Affairs Harborside Financial Center Plaza Three, Suite 602 Jersey City, New Jersey 07311 Dear Ms. Raposo: Please refer to your supplemental new drug applications dated and received April 4, 2008, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act for Lexapro (escitalopram oxalate) tablets (NDA 21-323), and Lexapro (escitalopram oxalate) solution (NDA 21-365) . These “Changes Being Effected” supplemental new drug applications provide for class labeling changes to the Events Reported Subsequent to the Marketing of Escitalopram Section of the Labeling: We completed our review of these applications, as amended. For administrative purposes, please designate this submission “SPL for approved supplement NDA 21- NDA 21-323/S-029 and NDA 21-365/S-019.” We remind you that you must comply with reporting requirements for an approved NDA (21 CFR 314.80 and 314.81).
