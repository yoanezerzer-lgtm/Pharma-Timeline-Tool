These Prior Approval supplemental new drug applications provide for revisions to the US Prescribing Information (USPI) as required according to Content and Format of Labeling for Human Prescription Drug and Biological Products; Requirements for Pregnancy and Lactation Labeling , referred to as the “Pregnancy and Lactation Labeling Rule” (PLLR, or final rule).
